Hi, welcome to my Ultimate Manager!

  In the game you start a career, choose your team and begin your managerial journey in the world of ultimate frisbee!
  Although based on UFA stats (thanks to their comprehensive stats page) it runs a different format - 16 team league + league cup

  I'm not gonna write about how everything works - feel free to explore every tab - there is a bunch of mechanics regarding everything (some of them are probably balanced better than others) trying to make it a (fairly) realistic frisbee world.
  Finances, player contracts, transfers, and world environment are based on football.
  Some stats/attributes/traits are generated randomly instead of being pulled from wherever.
  Yes, I know the media articles are cringe but I think it's still fun to have them x
  The game was vibe-coded so it might have some weird bugs I haven't caught yet - when I started doing it, I made it in Polish - technically the game should be localised in EN, but there might be certain elements that are not.
