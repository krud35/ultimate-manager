Hi, welcome to my Ultimate Manager!

  In the game you start a career, choose your team and begin your managerial journey in the world of ultimate frisbee!
Although based on UFA stats (thanks to their comprehensive stats page) it runs a different format -
16 team league + league cup

  I'm not gonna write about how everything works - feel free to explore every tab - 
there is a bunch of mechanics regarding everything (some of them are probably balanced better than others)
trying to make it a (fairly) realistic frisbee world.
  Finances, player contracts, transfers, and world environment are based on football.
Some stats/attributes/traits are generated randomly instead of being pulled from wherever.
Currently there is no mechanism of generating new players, so long-running careers might not work.
